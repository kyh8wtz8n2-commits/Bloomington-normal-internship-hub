How chapters work

Internship Hub uses a chapter structure inspired by professional organizations, but each chapter represents a local city or town. Chapters can share nearby internships, local employers, and career opportunities.

Bloomington and Normal were the first chapters. Peoria and Champaign have been added because current internship postings were found in those cities. Other towns can be requested and added when active local opportunities are confirmed.
